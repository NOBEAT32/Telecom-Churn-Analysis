Telecom Customer Churn — End-to-End Analysis & Prediction (Power BI + Python)

Built a complete churn analytics project on a 6,400+ customer telecom dataset, going from raw data to a business-ready dashboard.

What I did:
SQL + Python EDA — cleaned structural data issues, validated insights with chi-square tests, and broke down churn by contract, tenure, geography, and services
ML churn prediction — Logistic Regression, Random Forest & XGBoost (ROC-AUC ~0.89), with feature importance tied back to the EDA findings
Two Power BI dashboards — a Summary page for churn diagnostics and a Prediction page flagging 240 high-risk customers by revenue and tenure

Key findings:
- Month-to-month contracts drive 47% of churn vs just 3% for two-year plans
- Competitor switching is the #1 churn category (218 cases)
- Jammu & Kashmir has the highest state-level churn rate (57%)
- ₹1.6M+ in revenue sits with customers flagged as high churn-risk

The goal wasn't just to visualize churn — it was to build something a retention team could actually act on: who's at risk, why, and how much revenue is on the line.

Tools: Power BI, Python (pandas, scikit-learn, XGBoost), SQL
